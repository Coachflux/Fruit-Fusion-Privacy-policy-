Privacy Policy for Fruit Fusion

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Fruit Fusion ("the App") is developed by codexbrain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our servers. Game progress, settings, coins, and achievements are stored locally on your device only, using standard device storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed if you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect certain data from your device, which can include your advertising identifier and general device information. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review and control how Google uses this information, including opting out of personalized advertising, through your device's ad settings.

For more detail on how Google handles this data, see Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob-integrated apps

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If ads shown in the App are configured for a mixed or general audience, ad personalization may still be limited based on Google Play's family policies where applicable.

Data Sharing

Aside from the advertising data described above, which is collected and processed by Google's AdMob SDK, we do not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties.

Data Deletion

Since no personal data is stored on our servers, there is no account or server-side data for us to delete. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game data from your device. To manage or reset your advertising identifier, use your device's system settings (e.g. Settings → Privacy → Ads, on Android).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
